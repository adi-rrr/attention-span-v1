How it works

Share → reflect → get reminded.

One short journey, right from the share button you already use.

1

Understand

Share a post to Attention Span. It asks 2–4 short questions that adapt to the post and to your answers — like “Why did you save this?”

2

File & remind

You pick the folder — always. From your own answer it suggests a reminder time, and nothing is scheduled until you tap to confirm.

3

The reminder moment

When it's time, the save comes back with your reason right next to it. Do it, snooze it, or open the original.

AI & you

The AI asks. You decide.

We use AI where language needs understanding — and keep every decision about your stuff in your hands.

What the AI does

  • Asks adaptive follow-up questions, not a fixed survey.
  • Reads time hints in your answers (“two weeks before my trip”).
  • Drafts a reminder suggestion for you to confirm.

What stays yours

  • Choosing or creating folders — never auto-filed.
  • Confirming every reminder with one tap.
  • Marking things done, archiving or deleting.
The magic moment
“Want to eat here when I'm in Tokyo in March.”

Not a notification about a link — a note from past you, arriving exactly when it's useful.
