# Attention Span — Problem Statement & Elevator Pitch

## Problem statement

We save recipes, restaurants, articles and videos from Instagram, Reddit, Substack and YouTube so we can get to them later. Later never comes. Every save app asks for one tap and nothing else, so the "save" button turns into a pile of links we never open. Saving feels like progress, so we hoard instead of acting. And when we do want something back, we can't find it, because we've forgotten why we saved it.

## Sharpen Your Idea (3P: Person · Problem · Promise)

| Level | Person | Problem | Promise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** The broad version | Everyone online | Saves too much content | A place to keep it |
| **L2** Be specific | Heavy social-media users who save content across apps | Saves pile up and never get acted on | A save-for-later app that organizes them |
| **L3** Go to the extreme | Urban 18–30s who save places, recipes and reads from reels and feeds, then never go, cook or read | They forget *why* they saved something, so the link is dead weight and they can't find it again | Make them say why at the moment of saving, so they can search by their own words and get reminded to act |

**The L3 row is the business.**

## Elevator pitch

> I'm building **Attention Span** for **people who save things from Instagram, Reddit, YouTube and Substack and never come back to them**, who **lose the reason they saved something the second they save it**. Instead of another pile of links, we ask you one quick question when you save, *why does this matter?* After that you can search by your own words, get a reminder when it's time to act, and see saved places on a map. **You stop hoarding and start doing.**

### 20-second version

Everyone saves things they never act on. Save buttons are too easy, so saving replaces doing. Attention Span makes you pause for ten seconds and say why you saved it. Later, you find it by your own words and get nudged to act on it.

## Differentiation (one line)

Albo-style apps understand the link for you. Attention Span makes you understand why it matters, so the content stays yours.
